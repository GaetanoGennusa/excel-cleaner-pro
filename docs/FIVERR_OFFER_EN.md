# Fiverr listing draft — not published

**Title:** I will clean and standardize your Excel or CSV data with a change report

**Service:** Excel & CSV Data Cleaning

I will review one small spreadsheet and apply the cleaning rules we agree on. This can include removing extra spaces, making headings and text case consistent, converting Italian or international number formats, normalizing dates with a confirmed format, and removing exact duplicate rows when you authorize it.

You will receive a cleaned XLSX workbook with an issues sheet, a change log and metadata explaining the rules and checks. Uncertain values remain visible for your decision. Your source file is not overwritten. Processing takes place locally without paid AI services or a mandatory cloud account.

## Basic package

| Item | Proposed scope |
| --- | --- |
| Input | One valid CSV or unencrypted XLSX file; one selected worksheet |
| Size | Up to 1,000 data rows, 20 columns and 10 MB |
| Work | Supported cleaning rules agreed before the order |
| Delivery | Cleaned XLSX report, brief handover and optional cleaned CSV |
| Revision | One correction against the original agreed rules, columns and source file |
| Timing | Proposed two working days after file and requirements review; confirm availability before ordering |
| Price | Experimental EUR 25–40 range, to validate before publishing |

The price and delivery time are planning proposals, not evidence of market demand or guaranteed turnaround. Configure the eventual marketplace price in the currency and terms available to your account. Do not treat this draft as a published offer.

## Please provide before ordering

1. One sample or the intended file, shared through an agreed channel, with permission to process it. Use a redacted sample when possible.
2. The worksheet and columns to clean, and columns that must stay as text, such as customer codes or postal codes.
3. The number format and date format to use. Specify whether `01/02/2025` means 1 February or January 2.
4. Confirmation of whether exact duplicate rows may be removed, keeping the first copy.
5. The required output and a small example of an acceptable result.

Please discuss confidential or sensitive data and retention requirements before sending it. Local processing does not mean that sharing through a marketplace bypasses that platform's systems.

## Not included

VBA/macros, advanced financial analysis or accounting validation, corrupted-file repair, recovery of original formulas or charts, preservation of complex layouts, OCR, fuzzy duplicate merging, missing-value invention, enrichment, multiple files or worksheets, system integration or unlimited revisions.

A new file, new worksheet, additional columns or a changed interpretation of the data is a scope change, not the included revision. Unresolvable values will be reported rather than guessed. Delivery requires a manual comparison with the agreed source and acceptance criteria.
